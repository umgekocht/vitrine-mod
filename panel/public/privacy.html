Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Who we are

getvitrine.net is the community website of Vitrine, a free and open-source store for Minecraft. Contact: umgekocht@users.noreply.github.com.

What we collect

  • Google sign-in (optional): when you sign in, Google tells us your account ID, your e-mail address and your name. We use them only to recognise your account. Your e-mail address and Google name are never shown publicly.
  • Nickname: if you post suggestions, the nickname you choose is shown next to them.
  • Content you submit: uploaded packages, suggestions and votes. Approved packages are published openly in the Vitrine catalog on GitHub.
  • Technical data: your IP address is used for a short time to limit abuse (rate limits) and by Cloudflare to protect the site. We keep a log of moderation actions.

Cookies

We use only necessary cookies: one that remembers you passed the human check (12 hours) and, if you sign in, a session cookie (7 days). No advertising or tracking cookies, no analytics.

Service providers

  • Cloudflare (network, human check "Turnstile") – privacy policy
  • Google (sign-in) – privacy policy
  • GitHub (hosting of published packages) – privacy statement

How long we keep data

Account data is kept while your account exists. Rejected uploads are deleted right away. Backups are kept for 14 days. Rate-limit counters expire after at most 24 hours.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time to see, correct or delete your data, or to delete your account: write to umgekocht@users.noreply.github.com. You can also revoke access in your Google account settings.
